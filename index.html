Proof

Bound propagation over the network weights gives a proof, not a sample. For a stated range of fog, glare and lighting, the steering output stays inside a stated bound. Where the proof fails, you get the simulation worklist. Scope: camera-based steering networks. White paper: testing between the test cases

Safety case

One proof per ODD region, entered in the safety case as a coverage argument for the conditions between your test points. In ISO 21448 terms: simulate known triggering conditions, prove the space between them. Run it before the release gate.

Why now

In 2026 NHTSA upgraded its reduced-visibility crash investigation of a driver-assistance system to an engineering analysis, and a robotaxi was recalled over smoke it may miss. Visibility is a range. Testing covers it at points. NHTSA EA26002  ·  NHTSA recall 26E-044
